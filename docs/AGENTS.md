# The 41-Agent Investment Committee Directory

## Team 1: Stock Ranking (10 Agents)
1. **T1-01 Universe Screener Agent**: Filters broad market cap & liquidity universe.
2. **T1-02 Momentum Agent**: Analyzes 1M, 3M, 6M, 12M and Relative Strength.
3. **T1-03 Technical Trend Agent**: 50/200 EMA Golden Cross & Stage Analysis.
4. **T1-04 Quality Factor Agent**: ROE, ROIC, Piotroski F-Score.
5. **T1-05 Growth Factor Agent**: Revenue & EPS CAGR.
6. **T1-06 Value Factor Agent**: P/E, Forward P/E, EV/EBITDA, P/FCF.
7. **T1-07 Earnings Revision Agent**: Consensus upgrades/downgrades & EPS beat rate.
8. **T1-08 Volume & Liquidity Agent**: ADV and abnormal volume accumulation.
9. **T1-09 News & Catalyst Agent**: Product releases, contracts, M&A.
10. **T1-10 Ranking Chairman Agent**: Synthesizes factor score and selects Top 10-20.

## Team 2: Equity Research (10 Agents)
11. **T2-01 Revenue / EPS / Growth Agent**: Product segment breakdowns & margin expansion.
12. **T2-02 Income Statement Agent**: Line-by-line P&L analysis and operating leverage.
13. **T2-03 Balance Sheet Agent**: Net debt / EBITDA, solvency, quick ratio.
14. **T2-04 Cash Flow / FCF Agent**: Free cash flow conversion & buyback power.
15. **T2-05 Valuation Agent**: 2-stage DCF, Reverse DCF & Fair Value bands.
16. **T2-06 Business / Moat Agent**: Network effects, switching costs, cost moat.
17. **T2-07 Management Agent**: Capital allocation track record & insider skin in the game.
18. **T2-08 Earnings Call / Guidance Agent**: Transcript sentiment & guidance adjustments.
19. **T2-09 Industry / Competitor Agent**: TAM, market share trends, Porter's 5 Forces.
20. **T2-10 Research Chairman Agent**: Formulates Bull, Base, and Bear thesis.

## Team 3: Red Team Analysis (10 Agents)
21. **T3-01 Bear Case Agent**: Worst-case downside stress test.
22. **T3-02 Accounting / Forensic Agent**: Beneish M-Score, DSO spike, revenue recognition anomalies.
23. **T3-03 Valuation Challenge Agent**: Challenges multiple expansion and terminal growth assumptions.
24. **T3-04 Earnings Risk Agent**: Next-quarter estimate miss vulnerability.
25. **T3-05 Technical Breakdown Agent**: Distribution patterns, head & shoulders, support breaks.
26. **T3-06 Macro Risk Agent**: Interest rate sensitivity, FX, tariff, and geopolitical risks.
27. **T3-07 Industry Risk Agent**: Big tech threat, AI disruption, antitrust actions.
28. **T3-08 Crowded Trade / Sentiment Agent**: Short interest, retail euphoria, positioning squeeze.
29. **T3-09 Portfolio Correlation Agent**: Prevents covariance clustering in existing portfolio.
30. **T3-10 Red Team Chairman Agent**: Exercises binding `RED_TEAM_VETO` power.

32. **T4-02 Sector Allocation Agent**: Prevents sector concentration drift.
33. **T4-03 Portfolio Construction Agent**: Mean-variance & target volatility weighting.
34. **T4-04 Position Sizing Agent**: Fractional Kelly Criterion sizing.
35. **T4-05 Entry Timing Agent**: Pullback confirmation to demand zones.
36. **T4-06 Stop / Invalidation Agent**: ATR volatility stops & market structure invalidation.
37. **T4-07 Profit Target Agent**: Multi-tier TP1, TP2, TP3 targets.
38. **T4-08 Hedging Agent**: Cash buffer & protective strategies.
39. **T4-09 Execution / Slippage Agent**: TWAP/VWAP impact & slippage guardrails.
40. **T4-10 Portfolio Chairman Agent**: Compiles full Trade Plan for AI-CIO review.

## Supreme Council
